RIVERSIDE HOSTEL HANDBOOK (FICTIONAL SAMPLE DOCUMENT)

This handbook is invented for the DocChat demo. Riverside Hostel is not a real place.

1. Welcome
Welcome to Riverside Hostel. This handbook explains the rules and services that keep hostel life safe, clean and comfortable for every resident. Please read it fully during your first week and keep it for reference. If anything is unclear, speak to the warden, whose office is on the ground floor next to the main entrance.

2. Curfew and Entry
The main gate closes at 10:30 PM from Monday to Friday and on Sunday. On Saturday the curfew is extended to 11:30 PM. Residents returning after curfew must report to the security desk, give their room number and state the reason for the delay. Three late entries in one month will be reported to the warden. The gate opens again at 5:30 AM every day.

3. Mess Timings
The dining hall serves three meals a day. Breakfast is served from 7:30 AM to 9:00 AM. Lunch is served from 12:30 PM to 2:00 PM. Dinner is served from 7:30 PM to 9:30 PM. Residents who will miss a meal for more than three days in a row can apply for a mess rebate at the warden's office. Food may not be taken out of the dining hall in hostel plates.

4. Guests and Visitors
Guests are welcome in the ground-floor common room between 4:00 PM and 7:00 PM. Every guest must sign the visitor register at the main gate on arrival and on departure, and must show a photo ID. Guests are not permitted in residents' rooms or on the upper floors at any time. Residents are responsible for the behaviour of their guests while they are inside the hostel.

5. Wi-Fi and Electronics
Each student may connect a maximum of 2 devices to the hostel Wi-Fi network. Password resets are handled at the IT desk in Room G-12, which is open from 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM on weekdays. Cooking appliances such as induction plates and electric kettles are not allowed in rooms because they overload the wiring.

6. Laundry
Laundry service is available on Tuesdays and Fridays. Drop your clothes at the laundry room by 9:00 AM on those days, with your room number written on the bag. Clean clothes can be collected from 6:00 PM on the same day. The hostel is not responsible for items left uncollected for more than one week.

7. Fees
Hostel fees are due by the 10th of every month. A late fee of ₹50 per day is charged if hostel fees are paid after the 10th of the month. Payments can be made online through the student portal or at the accounts counter. Keep your payment receipt until the end of the semester.

8. Going Home and Leave
Residents who plan to stay away from the hostel overnight, including trips home, must submit a leave form to the warden at least 24 hours in advance. Leave forms are available at the warden's office and on the student portal. In an emergency, inform the security desk before leaving and submit the form as soon as possible.

9. Cleanliness and Quiet Hours
Rooms are inspected on the first Monday of every month. Keep corridors free of shoes and luggage. Quiet hours run from 11:00 PM to 6:00 AM; music and phone calls should be kept low so that others can sleep and study.
